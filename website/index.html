The Problem

Groundwater Depletion

Regions over-relying on continuous flooding are facing severe groundwater drops, threatening long-term agricultural sustainability.

AWD Adoption Failure

Farmers sharing a single pump cannot effectively adopt Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) because they are unable to coordinate schedules.

The Coordination Gap

Without a centralized system to decide when to pump and who gets water, communities face conflict or default back to continuous flooding.

The Solution

Sense

Sensor nodes in each plot monitor water levels autonomously.

Decide

The central coordinator prioritizes plots based on water stress and growth stage.

Act

Valves open and the pump is triggered automatically for the priority plot.

Confirm

Water usage is tracked and fairness credits are adjusted among farmers.

Key Features

Predictive Scheduling

Anticipates needs before critical thresholds are reached.

Equity & Water-Credit

Ensures fair distribution of water resources across all shared plots.

Growth-Stage Aware

Adapts irrigation based on the crop's current growth phase and rain forecasts.

Fail-Safe & Override

Shadow coordinator backup and manual override capabilities via SMS.

Solar Powered

Energy-independent field nodes suitable for remote deployment.

Modularity

Easily add or remove plots to the network dynamically.

Projected Impact

Quantifiable ecological and economic dividends unlocked through autonomous AWD field coordination.

Resource Efficiency

Substantial Water Savings

Replaces wasteful continuous submergence with dynamic soil-saturation cycling, conserving millions of liters while ensuring peak crop yield.

✓ Counters aquifer drop
Economic Relief

Lower Pump Fuel Costs

Coordinated queue-based dispatching eliminates idle pump cycles and slashes diesel generator consumption, directly reducing communal utility bills.

✓ Tangible farmer operating profit
Climate Action

Reduced Methane Emissions

Periodic aerobic field drying suppresses anaerobic methanogenic bacteria in rice roots, driving radical reductions in agricultural greenhouse gases.

✓ Verified carbon footprint cut
